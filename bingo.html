The rules:

Miss Candy will read out the numbers and you have to tap them on your sheet!

The first round is ONE LINE! This means when ONE horizontal line is completed in ANY of the five blocks from the read-out numbers YOU’RE THE WINNER of that round! SHOUT BINGO!

The second round is TWO LINES! This means when TWO horizontal lines are completed in ANY of the five blocks (even in different blocks) from the read-out numbers YOU’RE THE WINNER of that round! SHOUT BINGO!

The third round is a FULL HOUSE! This means when you complete a FULL BLOCK OF ALL THE NUMBERS from the read-out numbers YOU’RE THE WINNER of that round! SHOUT BINGO!

AFTER EVERY WINNER - TAP THE NEXT ROUND TYPE!

BINGO!

Congratulations you’re a winner!
